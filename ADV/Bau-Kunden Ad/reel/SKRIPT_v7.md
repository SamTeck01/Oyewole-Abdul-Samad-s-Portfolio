Marketingversprechen"). No fear claims, no made-up numbers, no promise of a fixed number of inquiries.

Notes
- Every claim is taken from the website (services, report fields, guarantee wording, cancel monthly, personal contact).
- Removed from v6: "Seite drei", "Die Aufträge gehen an die Konkurrenz" (not how the brand talks), price.
- Voice: open-source Chatterbox (MIT) cloned from a real human German narrator (LibriVox, public domain),
  not from a synthetic voice. Sample: `voice_test/neue_stimme_probe.mp3`.
